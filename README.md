### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a master's student at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**, working on efficient AI systems and computer vision. I'm particularly interested in **video generation**, **sparse attention**, and **inference optimization**.

Previously, I studied **Computer Science and Technology** at **Hangzhou Dianzi University (HDU)**, where I explored **infrared–visible image registration**.

- **Efficient AI:** sparse attention, KV-cache optimization, efficient inference
- **Computer Vision:** multimodal image registration, infrared–visible image alignment
